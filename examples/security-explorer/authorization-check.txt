AI027 — Wildcard tool or permission authorization [high]
  Detects: An authorization or tool allowlist explicitly grants a wildcard or all-tools capability. This may give an agent broader authority than its task requires.
  Remediation: Replace wildcard grants with named tools and constrained resources; enforce authorization server-side for each call and require approval for sensitive effects.
  CWE: CWE-732
  A recognized source pattern is a review signal, not proof of reachable exploitation, complete data flow, runtime protection or an acceptance-check pass.
