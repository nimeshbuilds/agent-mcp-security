# Invarune name research

**Decision date:** 19 September 2026  
**Chosen name:** Invarune  
**Parent brand:** NimeshBuild  
**Pronunciation:** IN-vuh-roon  
**Name idea:** A coined combination of *invariant* and *rune*: repeatable checks and readable evidence.  
**Tagline:** Evidence for agent security.

Invarune was selected after preliminary public searches found no exact-name match in the web, software registries, GitHub, or the basic US federal trademark search described below. Several other candidates had identifiable existing uses. Invarune had lower observed collision risk within this search scope; the research does **not** establish worldwide uniqueness, trademark clearance, or a right to use or register the name.

The accompanying [machine-readable research record](brand/name-screening-2026-09-19.json) preserves the registry responses, timestamps, search scope, decision, and limitations. Results describe what was observable on the research date. Searches and registry contents can change.

### US federal trademark search

A search was completed in the official [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results) interface with these settings:

- Search field: **Wordmark**.
- Search input: **Invarune**.
- Live and dead records included.
- Registered, pending, cancelled, and abandoned status categories selected.
- No class filter applied.
- Completed result shown: **“No results found.”**

This was a basic exact-name knockout search. It was not a comprehensive search for similar spellings, pronunciation, appearance, meaning, related goods or services, or likelihood of confusion. The linked search application may open its default state; it is not a permanent saved result.

The USPTO's [guidance on a comprehensive clearance search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) explains why a federal database search alone is insufficient and why similar marks and other sources must also be considered. The name selection here is a preliminary product naming decision, not a legal clearance opinion.

## Scope and limits

The following were **not** searched or completed:

- WIPO or other international and national trademark databases beyond the US federal search described above.
- US state trademark registers or state/business incorporation registers.
- A comprehensive common-law trademark search, professional clearance search, or legal assessment of confusing similarity.
- Every social network, app store, software registry, top-level domain, language, transliteration, or spelling variant.
- Nonpublic products, private repositories, pending unpublished uses, or future registrations.

No domain, package name, social handle, or trademark was purchased, reserved, filed, or registered as part of this work. The product uses **Invarune by NimeshBuild** without a registered-trademark symbol. Recheck the name before a wider commercial launch, registration, or material brand investment; the dated findings are a research record rather than an ongoing availability guarantee.

## Product naming decision

Use **Invarune** as the product name, **by NimeshBuild** as the parent-brand credit, and **Evidence for agent security.** as the tagline. The coined name describes the emphasis on repeatable security checks and inspectable evidence without making a claim that a scan proves security or eliminates false positives and false negatives.

Compatibility identifiers such as the existing Python package, distribution, rule IDs, and legacy CLI entry point may remain stable while the user-facing product adopts the new name. Those technical identifiers do not represent separate product brands.
